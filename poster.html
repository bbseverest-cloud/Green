Benefits of Green & Healthy Schools

01

PERFORMANCE

Student’s & building’s performance

Fresh air, daylight and an improved indoor environment enhance the performance of students. Water efficiency, energy efficiency and post-occupancy monitoring improve building performance.

02

PEDAGOGY

Science & art of education

Eco-sensitivity is both a passion and a science. Children get sensitised to environmental aspects as part of everyday learning.

03

COMMUNITY

Help to educate the greater community

Knowledge sharing within the school helps in reaching out to parents and nearby communities.

04

RESPONSIBILITY

Towards the environment

Children learn to take responsibility for their own actions that concern the environment.
