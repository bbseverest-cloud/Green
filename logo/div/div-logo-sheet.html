दिव्

Div: to shine, to illumine, to radiate

A diya carries all three meanings of the Sanskrit root. Its flame shines, the lamp exists to illumine the space around it, and its light radiates outward. Moulded rims at the top and foot give the lamp a crafted, three-dimensional feel, and the flat rim echoes the shirorekha, the headline stroke that joins the letters of दिव्.

To shine

The flame: the energy and brilliance we bring to every project.

To illumine

The diya: we hold the light so our clients’ spaces glow.

To radiate

The rays: care and value that reach well beyond handover.
