Your clients want spaces that are healthier and cheaper to run. The IGBC Green Interiors rating gives them proof, and gives your practice a certified green offering. We join your team at the design stage and handle the certification end to end, so you can keep designing.
About the rating
The IGBC Green Interiors rating programme is designed for the specific needs of tenant-occupied commercial spaces. Owner-occupied spaces can apply too, provided these requirements have not already been addressed in the main building.
