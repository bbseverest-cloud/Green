Deliver IGBC-certified interiors, from design stage to handover

Your clients want spaces that are healthier and cheaper to run. The IGBC Green Interiors rating gives them proof, and gives your practice a certified green offering. We join your team at the design stage and handle the certification end to end, so you can keep designing.

ABOUT THE RATING
The IGBC Green Interiors rating programme is designed for the specific needs of tenant-occupied commercial spaces. Owner-occupied spaces can apply too, provided these requirements have not already been addressed in the main building.

PROJECTS WE CERTIFY
Ideally suited to commercial interiors, though not limited to them:
- Retail spaces, showrooms & malls
- Restaurants & food joints
- Office & IT space fit-outs
- Large commercial interiors
- Hotels, resorts & small hospitality
- Bank branches
- Clinics
- Other commercial spaces

END-TO-END, ONE POINT OF CONTACT
1. Design stage: we join early, set the certification target with you and fold the green requirements into the design brief.
2. Registration: we register the project with IGBC under the Green Interiors rating.
3. GreenPro product selection: we help you choose GreenPro-certified materials and products that fit your design and budget.
4. Certification & handover: we prepare the documentation and see the project through to certification, ready for handover.

WHAT YOUR CLIENTS GAIN
- 30–40% lower energy cost
- 20–30% lower water requirement
- Better indoor air quality, human-centric lighting, non-toxic materials, superior acoustics and ergonomics, and healthier, happier occupants
