About the rating

Built for commercial interiors

Tenant-occupied commercial spaces

The IGBC Green Interiors rating programme is designed for the specific requirements of tenant fit-outs.

Owner-occupied spaces too

They qualify as well, provided these requirements have not already been addressed in the main building.

Ideally suited, not limited

Office fit-outs, malls, retail, hotels, restaurants, resorts, IT spaces, banks and other buildings.
