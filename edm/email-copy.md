IGBC GREEN INTERIORS RATING
Healthy spaces. Smarter resources.

DESIGN INTERIORS THAT CARE FOR PEOPLE & PLANET

We spend 90% of our time indoors, so the look, air quality and comfort of interior spaces matter more than ever. Designers around the world now plan interiors around these elements, and the IGBC Green Interiors rating recognises the ones that get it right.

Green interiors also address national concerns: water and energy efficiency, lower fossil-fuel use, handling of consumer waste and conserving natural resources.

MULTIFOLD BENEFITS
- 30–40% reduction in energy cost
- 20–30% reduction in water requirement
- Enhanced indoor air quality
- Human-centric lighting
- Non-toxic materials
- Superior acoustics & ergonomics
- Improved health & well-being of occupants

WHAT THE RATING COVERS
01 Eco Design Approach
02 Water Conservation
03 Energy Efficiency
04 GreenPro Materials
05 Indoor Environment
06 Innovation in Interior Design
07 Health & Wellness

IDEAL FOR
Retail, commercial spaces, food courts, restaurants, hospitality, health care, and fully furnished dwellings by developers.

FOR INTERIOR DESIGNERS: PROMOTE GREEN INTERIORS AS YOUR USP
Stand out with clients who want healthier spaces and lower running costs. An IGBC-rated interior is proof you can point to.
